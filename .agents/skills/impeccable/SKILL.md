# Impeccable Design & Engineering Standards

The `impeccable` skill ensures every interface, component, and visual interaction meets high-end, authored design standards.

---

## Core Principles

1. **Authored Aesthetic over AI Slop**:
   - Avoid generic AI-generated templates, floating glowing 3D objects, neon gradients, excessive rounded card spam, and decorative UI without purpose.
   - Use intentional composition, strong typography hierarchies, thoughtful whitespace, and authentic content.

2. **Typography & Layout Precision**:
   - Pair curated typefaces (e.g. editorial serifs with clean geometric sans or mono tags).
   - Maintain fluid typography (`clamp()`) and consistent vertical rhythm.
   - Ensure high contrast and WCAG AA accessibility standards across all themes.

3. **Motion & Interaction Quality**:
   - Use subtle, physics-based micro-interactions (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`).
   - Ensure hover, focus-visible, active, and scroll-triggered transitions feel tactile and responsive.
   - Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` everywhere.

4. **Code Structure & Execution**:
   - Zero broken imports, zero console warnings, zero layout shifts.
   - Clean, modular component architecture with strict TypeScript types.
   - Always verify implementations with production builds (`npm run build`).

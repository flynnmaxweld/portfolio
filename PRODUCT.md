# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Potential clients evaluating Flynn Maxwell's ability to design and build thoughtful digital products.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio presents selected software and creative technology work so visitors can understand Flynn's capabilities and choose to start a conversation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The site is a React/Vite portfolio with dedicated Hero, About, Work, and Contact sections. The Work section must preserve project detail, imagery, external links, and the in-development note where provided.

## Evidence on Hand

Project imagery and factual project descriptions live in `public/images/` and `src/components/Work.tsx`. No testimonials or client metrics are provided and must not be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Show the work before explaining it.
- Make technical depth legible without making the visitor work for it.
- Keep project claims grounded in the supplied descriptions.
- Make the next step toward contact obvious.
